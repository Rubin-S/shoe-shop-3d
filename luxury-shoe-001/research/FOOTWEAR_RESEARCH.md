# Luxury Footwear Engineering & Architecture Dossier

**Artifact Target**: `research/FOOTWEAR_RESEARCH.md`  
**Purpose**: Synthesize the design grammar, anatomical lasts, physical construction methods, and material physics of world-class bespoke men's footwear (John Lobb, Gaziano & Girling, Edward Green, Berluti, Corthay).

---

## 1. Formal Shoe Typologies & Silhouette Grammar

### 1.1 Taxonomy of Men's Luxury Footwear
- **Oxford (Balmoral)**: The pinnacle of formal elegance. Defined by **closed lacing**, where the quarters are stitched beneath the vamp. Creates a seamless, streamlined instep with a characteristic 'V' gap.
- **Wholecut Oxford**: Cut from a single continuous piece of premium calfskin with only one seam at the heel. Demands the absolute highest grade of leather (zero grain flaws) and aggressive lasting tension.
- **Derby (Blucher)**: **Open lacing**, quarters stitched on top of the vamp. More forgiving instep fit, slightly more casual.
- **Double Monk Strap**: Functional brass/palladium buckles over an integrated leather bridge strap.

### 1.2 Last Geometry & Spatial Proportions
The **last** is the wooden or composite sculpted form around which the shoe is molded.
- **Silhouette Philosophy**: Modern bespoke British/Continental hybrid—subtle soft-chisel toe with a sculpted waist and pinched heel counter.
- **Toe Proportions**:
  - Toe spring: Elevated 12–15mm off the ground plane to facilitate natural foot roll without toe scuffing.
  - Toe cap length: Exactly 28%–31% of the total shoe length.
- **Vamp & Instep Geometry**:
  - Throat line: Positioned at 45%–48% from the toe tip.
  - Instep rise: 25°–28° incline from the throat apex to the collar rim.
- **Waist Arch (Fiddleback / Beveled Waist)**:
  - Medial and lateral waist width aggressively skived and pinched inwards by 22%–28% relative to forefoot ball width.
  - Spine ridge: Triangular convex camber beneath the midfoot arch, concealing the steel/carbon shank.
- **Heel Geometry**:
  - Total stacked height: 26mm–28mm (classic formal pitch).
  - Forward rake / Pitch: Heel counter sits perpendicular to the ground, while the heel block features a subtle 2° forward taper toward the waist.

## 2. Structural Construction Methods

### 2.1 Hand-Welted vs. Goodyear Welted (360° vs. 270°)
- **270° Beveled Goodyear / Hand-Welted**:
  - The welt runs from the lateral ball, around the toe, to the medial ball (270° perimeter).
  - The waist is trimmed flush and blind-stitched or pegged, creating an ultra-sleek, tailored profile with zero sole flare beneath the arch.
- **Inseam Construction**:
  - Hand-carved holdfast directly into a 4.5mm vegetable-tanned leather insole (eliminating synthetic gemming canvas).
  - Heavy waxed linen thread binding upper, lining, and welt at 6–8 SPI (Stitches Per Inch).

### 2.2 Outsole & Midsole Architecture
- **Outsole Material**: 5mm Oak-bark tanned leather (slow-tanned for 12–14 months, e.g., J. & F.J. Baker / Joh. Rendenbach).
- **Stitch Density & Concealed Channel**:
  - Lockstitched outsole rapid seam at **10–12 SPI**.
  - **Closed Channel**: A thin 1mm flap of sole leather is skived back, the rapid stitch is sewn into the groove, and the flap is cemented back down and burnished with hot bone tools to create a completely smooth, stitch-free bottom sole.
- **Fudge Wheel Detail**:
  - The top surface of the welt receives decorative wheeled indentations (fudging) at exactly matching 10–12 SPI, visually framing the upper.

### 2.3 Heel Stack Architecture
- **Lifts**: 4 to 5 precision-cut oak-bark leather lifts (each 4.5mm–5mm thick), skived flat and compressed.
- **Top Piece (Dovetail Quarter-Rubber)**:
  - 65% oak-bark leather with a 35% vulcanized natural rubber dovetail insert at the lateral rear strike zone.
  - Secured with grouped solid brass nails (clustered in 3s or 5s) countersunk into the leather.

## 3. Materials, Tanning & Micro-Surface Physics

### 3.1 Upper Leather Specifications
- **Full-Grain French Box Calf (Tanneries d'Annonay / Le Puy)**:
  - Thickness: 1.2mm – 1.4mm.
  - Micro-Structure: Tight cellular hair-follicle grain with natural micro-crevices; zero synthetic polymer coating.
  - PBR Properties:
    - Base Color: Deep aniline dye penetration (not surface pigment).
    - Roughness: Dual-scale—macro smooth (0.28–0.35) with micro-crevice pore roughness (0.50–0.65).
    - Clearcoat / Wax Sheen: Hand-rubbed carnauba wax topcoat (clearcoat: 0.25–0.40, clearcoatRoughness: 0.15).

### 3.2 Interior Lining
- **Vegetable-Tanned Drum-Dyed Calfskin**:
  - Natural glove-soft temper (0.8mm–1.0mm thickness).
  - Breathable, moisture-wicking natural beige/cognac tone.
  - Seamless heel pocket with reverse suede counter grip to prevent heel slippage.

### 3.3 Edge Inking & Burnishing
- Sole edges and heel stack are hand-scraped, stained with aniline dye, and burnished using heated iron wheels with beeswax and carnauba wax to achieve a glass-smooth mirror edge with defined wheeling lines.

## 4. Lighting & Visual Presentation Standards

### 4.1 Master Studio Lighting Rig (Cycles / PBR)
- **Key Light**: 2m × 1m softbox overhead, tilted 45° forward, providing continuous specular gradient along the vamp crest.
- **Rim Kickers**: Dual narrow strip lights at 135° left/right to define the waist pinch and collar rim silhouette against dark backgrounds.
- **Ground Bounce**: Warm reflective cyclorama bounce fill (0.15 intensity) to illuminate the fiddleback sole contour.
- **Camera Focal Length**: 90mm – 105mm telephoto equivalent to eliminate perspective distortion and maintain true anatomical proportions.

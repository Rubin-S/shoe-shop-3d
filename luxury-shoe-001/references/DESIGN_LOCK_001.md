## 2. Selection Rationale

**Winner: Candidate 01 (Aurelius Cap-Toe Oxford)**
1. **Modular Customization Architecture**: The distinct cap-toe seam, 5-pair closed lacing throat, quarter panel junctions, and 270° fudged welt allow granular, independent runtime customization in WebGL (upper leather, toe burnish/patina, welt stitch, sole stain, and laces).
2. **Exemplary Bespoke Proportions**: The 29% toe cap ratio, 14mm toe spring, athletic fiddleback waist pinch, and stacked leather heel with brass nail array establish the highest echelon of footwear craft.
3. **PBR Material Fidelity**: Deep French Box Calf with natural cellular pore structure and dual-tone hand-rubbed obsidian shadow patina at the toe.

---

## 3. Design Invariant Directives for 3D Modeling

- **Toe Cap**: Soft-chisel contour with 29% proportional length and mirror glaze transition.
- **Lacing**: Closed Oxford facing with 5 blind eyelets and 2mm flat-round braided waxed laces.
- **Welt**: 270° perimeter with 12 SPI wheeled fudging and close-cut waist.
- **Waist**: Sculpted fiddleback triangular ridge concealing the arch shank.
- **Heel**: 4 compressed leather lifts + 1 rubber dovetail strike piece secured with brass nail clusters.
